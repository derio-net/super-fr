<!-- fr:journal kind=decision scope=plan id=p1-status-semantics created=2026-10-22T07:56:12+00:00 phase=1 -->
### p1-status-semantics · decision · How fr ops status reads the less obvious cases (phase 1)

`check: proof` reports DONE (proof), a label beside checked/attested/legacy.
A legacy `status: done` closes the op before any probe runs. `na_if` and
`escalate` run once with no identity env; `check` runs once per identity.
An op with `disables` never runs `na_if`. ESCALATE applies to OPEN,
UNVERIFIABLE and CANNOT-VERIFY, never to N/A. A checkless op is closed only
by an `attested` entry (a recorded `done` does not close it). Reasons are
cut to 200 chars and every identity value in them is masked. `status`
orders ops by `needs` and refuses (exit 2) a cycle or an unknown id.
