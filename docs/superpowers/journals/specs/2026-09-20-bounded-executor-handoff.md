### d2-collapse-rule · decision · d2 Collapse rule: state-first, not dependency-only

compose_handoff (journal/model.py:363) renders an entry in full when 'e.phase is None or e.phase in relevant'. Effective state is consulted only to route a finding into the Open findings section; a FIXED finding tagged to a dependency phase still renders in full. Measured on docs/superpowers/implemented/journals/plans/2026-09-18-harness-parity-matrix.md: phase 6 re-read ~30 resolved findings (~30k chars). Decision: an entry's effective state decides first — fixed/refuted findings collapse to one line even in dependency phases; open findings, decisions and discoveries stay full when dependency-relevant. Rejected as the primary rule: a hard size budget (can drop something a phase needed); kept on the table only as a possible backstop, which the operator did not select.

### cc-transcript-shape · discovery · Claude Code transcripts do carry per-request token usage — V2 is feasible, verified on a live file

Checked 2026-09-20 against a live transcript under ~/.claude/projects/<cwd-slug>/<session-id>.jsonl (361 records). ### spec-selfreview-overclaim · finding [fixed] · Spec self-review caught the draft asserting a bound it cannot deliver

The first draft's A3 asserted 'handoff size does not grow monotonically with phase number' and estimated phase 6 at ~25k. Categorising the real phase-6 handoff refuted both. Measured: fixed findings 26 entries/37,556 chars (collapsible); norefactor non-dependency 5/3,515 (collapsible); norefactor dependency-phase 9/6,557 (NOT collapsible under d3); open findings 4/6,339 and decisions+discoveries 22/28,298 (both correctly retained). After-figures are therefore ~46k at phase 6 and ~24k at phase 1 — a 45% cut, but still 1.9x, because what remains carries genuine forward value. Fixed by replacing the bar with the property that is true and load-bearing: a closed entry contributes O(1) chars regardless of body size. Also added A4 stating the d3 residual openly rather than letting the 45% figure imply the bookkeeping problem was fully solved.
